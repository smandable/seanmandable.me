### What Debt Descent is

Debt Descent helps you plan how to pay off your debts. You enter your debts, balances, rates, and payments (or read them from a statement on your device), pick a payoff method like Snowball or Avalanche, and the app shows you a plan and an estimated debt-free date. You can log payments and watch your progress against that plan.

The app doesn't connect to your bank or your lenders. It doesn't move money, make payments, or talk to anyone on your behalf. Everything it knows about your debts comes from you: what you type in, import, or confirm from a statement.

### It's a planning tool, not advice

Debt Descent is not financial, legal, or tax advice, and I'm not a financial advisor, credit counselor, lawyer, or tax professional. The app does math on the numbers you give it. It can't know your whole situation. When it shows what to pay on each debt, that's what the payoff method you picked would do, not a recommendation for you.

Before you make a big decision, like stopping a payment, consolidating, refinancing, or enrolling in a debt management plan, please talk to your lender or a qualified professional.

Changing your plan in the app doesn't change what you owe, and it doesn't tell your lender anything. A missed payment can still bring late fees, extra interest, or a mark on your credit, depending on your lender's terms.

### Projections are estimates

The debt-free date, interest totals, payoff order, and every other projection in the app are estimates. They're only as good as what you enter, and your lender's real math can differ from the app's model. Here are a few things that can make them differ:

- how your lender calculates interest (average daily balance, posting dates, compounding)
- fees, penalty rates, and rate changes
- promotional rates, deferred interest, and when they end
- payments that post on a different day, or for a different amount, than you planned

When the statement reader or the paystub reader fills in a field, check it against the page before you save. The app shows you every field for this reason. Your lender's statement is always the final word on what you owe. The free calculators on this site are estimates in the same way.

### Your data

Your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud. I never receive it, and I can't see it. The [Privacy Policy](/debt-descent/privacy/) has the details.

That also means I can't recover your data for you. iCloud sync keeps your devices in step, but it isn't a backup. Deleting a debt removes it everywhere. Keep your own backup with **Export to JSON** now and then.

Files you export are yours to look after. A JSON backup carries your whole plan, so only send it to people you'd share your plan with.
